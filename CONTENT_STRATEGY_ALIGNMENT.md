## 🧱 Content Sub-Niches (Agent Assignable)

All content must fit one of these buckets:

1. **Global → India Impact**
   - US, China, Europe events → Indian markets, jobs, inflation
   - Example: "How China's Economic Slowdown Will Impact Indian Manufacturing Jobs by 2027"

2. **India Economic Futures (2026–2030)**
   - Markets, banking, employment, consumption, savings
   - Example: "India's Banking Sector Transformation: What It Means for Household Savings by 2028"

3. **RBI, Policy & Budget — Explained Simply**
   - What policies mean, not what they are
   - Example: "RBI's Digital India Initiative: How It Will Change Banking in Tier-2 Cities by 2026"

4. **Sector-Specific Deep Dives**
   - Banking, fintech, MSMEs, manufacturing, energy
   - Example: "India's Fintech Revolution: Impact on Traditional Banking Jobs (2026–2030)"

5. **Regional India Finance**
   - Tier-2, Tier-3, state-level impacts
   - Example: "How Maharashtra's Economic Policies Will Impact Rural Banking Access by 2027"

## 🚫 Content Rejection Criteria

**Reject content if it does NOT:**
- ✅ Explain what it means for India in the future
- ✅ Focus on impact over the next 1–5 years
- ✅ Provide clear, practical interpretation
- ✅ Fit one of the 5 content buckets above

**Reject content if it:**
- ❌ Is breaking news or generic summaries
- ❌ Provides stock tips or buy/sell recommendations
- ❌ Lacks future impact analysis
- ❌ Doesn't explain implications for Indians
- ❌ Is hype or predictions without logic

## 🎯 Content Quality Standards

### Article Structure (Per Master Prompt)
1. **H1:** Clear India-focused headline (include year if relevant)
2. **Intro:** Why this matters for India now
3. **Context:** Brief background of the event
4. **India Impact Analysis:**
   - Economy
   - Inflation / Interest Rates
   - Jobs / Consumption
   - Markets / Banking
   - Short-term vs Long-term Implications
5. **What Indians Should Understand** (not advice)
6. **Key Takeaways** (bullet points)

### Length & Quality
- **1,200–1,800 words** per article
- **Future-oriented** (2026–2030 focus)
- **India-specific** analysis
- **No stock tips** or breaking news
- **Suitable for long-form SEO**

### Authority & Trust
- Cite credible sources (RBI, IMF, World Bank, govt data)
- Maintain neutral, analytical tone
- No buy/sell language
- Clear disclaimers (not financial advice)
